# Privacy Policy for Smart Toolkit - All in One

**Last updated:** September 22, 2026

**Smart Toolkit - All in One** ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an offline-first utility application. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information, files, and device permissions.

---

## 1. Summary of Core Principles
* **Offline Processing:** All core utility tools (Photo Compressor, Image to PDF, QR Scanner, QR Generator, Video Compressor, Document Scanner, Watermark Photo, File Size Calculator, Unit Converter, Text to QR Code, Photo Resizer) run entirely on your local device.
* **No File Uploads:** We do **NOT** transmit, upload, or store your photos, videos, documents, or QR content on any external servers. Your personal files remain solely on your device.
* **No Account Required:** You can use all features of the App without creating an account or providing your name, phone number, or email address.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Information Processed Locally on Your Device
When you use tools in the App:
* **Photos & Videos:** Processed in local device memory for compression, resizing, watermarking, and PDF conversion.
* **Camera Input:** The camera feed is processed in real time solely to scan QR codes and capture documents. Frames are never sent to external servers.
* **Calculations & QR Content:** Data entered into calculators or QR generators is processed instantly on your device.

### B. Information Collected by Third-Party Partners (Google AdMob)
The App displays advertisements provided by **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to support free development. Google AdMob may collect and process certain diagnostic and device data, including:
* Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID)
* IP address (used for approximate geographic location for ad targeting)
* Ad interaction data (views, clicks, impressions)
* App diagnostics and crash reports

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google uses information from apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

## 3. Device Permissions and Why We Need Them

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)** | Used exclusively for the QR Code Scanner and Document Scanner PDF tools to scan codes and photograph documents on demand. |
| **Storage / Media (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`)** | Used exclusively to let you choose photos and videos for compression, resizing, watermarking, or PDF conversion, and to save the processed outputs to your device storage. |
| **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`)** | Used solely by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display banner and interstitial ads. |

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time through your Android device settings (**Settings > Apps > Smart Toolkit > Permissions**).

---

## 4. Data Retention and Security
* Because your files and utility operations are processed entirely on-device, we do not possess or retain any of your files or personal data.
* Temporary cache files generated during compression or PDF conversion can be wiped at any time directly inside the App (**Settings > Clear Cache**).

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through third-party ad networks, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.
